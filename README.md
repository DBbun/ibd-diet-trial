# My IBD Diet Trial

A free, offline-first web app that guides one person through a structured elimination and
reintroduction diet trial for Crohn's disease or ulcerative colitis, and shows what actually
changed for them.

- Everything is stored in the browser. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no
  tracking of any kind. Entries never leave the device.
- Symptoms are scored with the Harvey-Bradshaw Index (Crohn's) and the partial Mayo score
  (ulcerative colitis). Lab values are optional and sit outside the trial's conclusion.
- Results can be exported, including as FHIR, to share with a clinician.

**This app is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.** It does not diagnose or
treat anything. Decisions about treatment stay with the person and their care team.

Phase 1 prototype by Uri Kartoun, PhD (DBbun LLC) for the NIH NOURISH Autoimmunity Digital
Health Challenge.
